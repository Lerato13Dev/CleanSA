How CleanScore works

CleanScore (0–100) estimates environmental pressure at a location from data already in the system — it is a transparent formula, not a prediction.

  • Severity (35%) — average severity of reports at that location.
  • Persistence (25%) — share of reports still unresolved.
  • Volume (25%) — number of reports, capped at 6+.
  • Age (15%) — how long unresolved reports have been outstanding, capped at 45 days.

Risk bands: 0–24 Low · 25–49 Moderate · 50–74 High · 75–100 Critical. Verification status affects confidence in the data, not the score itself.
